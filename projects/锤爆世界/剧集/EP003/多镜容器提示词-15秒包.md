### VO-02 冯彩虹（男中高｜5.0 字/秒｜音量中）

Resonance sits deep in the chest with little nasal colour. Delivery is slow and level, roughly four characters per second, with generous pauses at punctuation; volume is moderate and never pushed. Emotionally flat and matter-of-fact — the same tone for offering noodles and for threatening to break a man's legs. Dry, almost amused, never loud. Avoid: announcer polish, radio-magnet smoothness, breathless excitement, rising sentence endings, theatrical menace.
